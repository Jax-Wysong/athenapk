Three-dimensional Berta C1 MHD blast wave
==========================================

Midplane diagnostics at ``z = 0`` for the C1 blast from Berta et al. (2024),
Section 5.7. The initially spherical over-pressured region expands anisotropically in
the uniform oblique magnetic field. The report includes the density evolution and
final density, logarithmic thermal pressure, specific kinetic energy, and magnetic
energy density at ``t = 0.2``.
